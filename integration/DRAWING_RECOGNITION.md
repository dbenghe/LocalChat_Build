# Conservative drawing recognition

The article table supplies candidate identities, not proof of presence or placement. A shared caption or colour must not approve every table row. Qualified codes retain their suffixes: 3000 and 3001(rd) are distinct, and no family alias is implied.

## Orthographic rectangular profiles

The native-vector matcher recognizes closed front-view profiles sharing exact X endpoints with a separate closed orthographic projection. A printed profile dimension must be connected to both contour endpoint ticks. An independent larger wall contour and its printed height/ordinates must agree with the calibration. The other profile dimension is **derived from aligned vector geometry**, rather than claimed as a separately printed measurement. X placement requires endpoint ordinate values on a connected dimension chain; width alone is insufficient. The report labels this geometric inference explicitly.

Numeric text on an unticked leader extension may belong to the first measurement interval. Text within another bounded interval or behind an intervening tick cannot be borrowed. Contradictory readings remain unresolved.

Automatic association requires unique table ownership of the profile dimensions, a positive integral quantity, exactly that many unique matching contours, and complete placement evidence for every occurrence. Repeated CAD painting is deduplicated. Two different position codes with identical profiles are not assigned to arbitrary occurrences or divided by quantity. Missing contours, projection, reference, ordinates, quantities or individual associations preserve manual review. This supplement does not bypass the engine's unknown-layout guard or identify unlabelled anchors/screw sleeves.

## Validation and delivery

Synthetic regression cases are in test_profile_projection.py. Real project PDFs, images, per-page findings and output paths remain in the local audit, outside Git. Shared recognition corrections are applied independently to the owned LocalChat engine and the separate PDFCONTROL engine, with backups and preservation of unrelated legacy modifications. Regenerate controls from original PDFs, never from the previously annotated outputs. An unresolved recognition warning is not proof that the item is physically absent.
