# Conservative drawing recognition

The article table supplies candidate identities, not proof of presence or placement. A shared caption or colour must not approve every table row. Qualified codes retain their suffixes: 3000 and 3001(rd) are distinct, and no family alias is implied.

## Orthographic rectangular profiles

The native-vector matcher recognizes closed front-view profiles sharing exact X endpoints with a separate closed orthographic projection. A printed profile dimension must be connected to both contour endpoint ticks. Calibration is read independently from the larger wall contour and its unambiguous printed height/endpoint ordinates, before comparison with small profile dimensions. Rounded vector coordinates on a 20–36 mm contour do not determine the global drawing scale. The other profile dimension is **derived from aligned vector geometry**, rather than claimed as a separately printed measurement. X placement requires endpoint ordinates or a complete path of explicitly measured adjacent cells connected to a wall boundary. Each cell must have real endpoint ticks and one unambiguous value consistent with the independent scale; disconnected internal widths, skipped cells and contradictory readings cannot establish position. Width alone is insufficient. The report labels this geometric inference explicitly.

Numeric text on an unticked leader extension may belong to the first measurement interval. Text within another bounded interval or behind an intervening tick cannot be borrowed. Contradictory readings remain unresolved. A scalar value on a subdivided chain cannot be borrowed for the full span. Staggered horizontal dimensions use a bounded font-relative distance, with the nearest parallel stroke considered; rotated vertical dimensions must face their own dimension column rather than a neighbour. Unsupported placement stays manual.

Automatic association requires unique table ownership of the profile dimensions, a positive integral quantity, exactly that many unique matching contours, and complete placement evidence for every occurrence. Repeated CAD painting is deduplicated. Two different position codes with identical profiles are not assigned to arbitrary occurrences or divided by quantity. Missing contours, projection, reference, ordinates, quantities or individual associations preserve manual review. This supplement does not bypass the engine's unknown-layout guard or identify unlabelled anchors/screw sleeves.

## Validation and delivery

Synthetic regression cases are in test_profile_projection.py. Real project PDFs, images, per-page findings and output paths remain in the local audit, outside Git. Shared recognition corrections are applied independently to the owned LocalChat engine and the separate PDFCONTROL engine, with backups and preservation of unrelated legacy modifications. Regenerate controls from original PDFs, never from the previously annotated outputs. An unresolved recognition warning is not proof that the item is physically absent.

## Recognized geometry with an unresolved question

The report distinguishes an unidentified caption from a recognized profile whose X placement is not completely demonstrated. The latter retains a CONTROLEREN finding on the affected contour and names the unconfirmed edge. Where complete jointly dimensioned geometry matches the combined quantity of several identical profile codes, the report preserves each code's unresolved assignment; quantity alone cannot establish which code belongs to which occurrence. Neither condition is automatically promoted to a proven drawing defect, and neither is silently approved.

Unlabelled screw sleeves, anchors and transport beams require an independently established identity/symbol convention before borrowing dimensions or granting an implicit-parent exemption. Shared colour, a matching count or a nearby differently named article does not establish that identity.

For an unrecognized article identity, X/Y are unknown rather than asserted missing. The CONTROLEREN finding remains. Explanatory recognition counts include complete independent geometric recognition; they do not change position-code ambiguity into an individual identity assignment.
